# Audio license

All audio here is released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal,
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not
required; it is given here anyway.

- `sfx/`: [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) by Kenney
  (www.kenney.nl), renamed:
  - `auto-cannon-0` to `-2` are `laserSmall_000` to `_002`, and
    `enemy-shot` is `laserSmall_004`;
  - `big-space-gun-0` and `-1` are `laserLarge_000` and `_001`, and
    `teleport` is `laserLarge_002`;
  - `zapper-0` to `-2` are `laserRetro_000` to `_002`;
  - `shield` is `forceField_000`, `field-zap-0` to `-2` are
    `forceField_001`, `_003` and `_004`, and `charge` is `forceField_002`;
  - `rocket-blast` is `explosionCrunch_000`, `enemy-explosion` is
    `explosionCrunch_002`, and `big-blast` is `lowFrequency_explosion_001`;
  - `part-switch` is `impactMetal_004`;
  - `engine-base` is `spaceEngineSmall_000`, `engine-big-pulse` is
    `spaceEngine_000`, `engine-burst` is `engineCircular_000`, and
    `engine-supercharged` is `spaceEngineLarge_000`;
  - `rocket-launch` is the first 0.45 s of `thrusterFire_000`, faded in over
    20 ms and out over its last 0.3 s.

  The `.ogg` files are re-encoded from Kenney's Ogg Vorbis originals to Ogg
  Opus, 64 kbit/s mono, with the metadata stripped (#269); the stereo
  originals have identical channels. Each sound also has an MP3 copy for
  browsers without Ogg support.
- `music/`: [5 Chiptunes (Action)](https://opengameart.org/content/5-chiptunes-action)
  by Juhani Junkala: Title Screen, Level 1 to 3 and Ending, renamed and
  re-encoded to Ogg Opus (96 kbit/s) and MP3 (112 kbit/s).
